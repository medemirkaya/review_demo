# Attribution

All video in this repository's published site is excerpted from open movies produced by the
Blender Foundation / Blender Institute. The excerpts were shortened, scaled to at most 480p and
re-encoded to H.264/AAC (HLS with MPEG-TS segments, one raw MPEG-TS file, and MP4 files). No other
changes were made. The original files are not stored in this repository; `scripts/build_media.py`
fetches them from the official Blender download server when the site is built.

Logos, trademarks and cover art of the films are **not** covered by the licenses and are not used.
